# Dash Dodge — Store Listing Draft

## Short description
Dodge, weave, collect coins, and chase your highest score in a fast offline endless runner.

## Full description
Dash Dodge is a fast, one-thumb endless runner built for quick sessions.

Switch between three lanes, dodge incoming obstacles, collect coins, and use temporary Shield and Magnet power-ups to survive longer. As your speed increases, every lane change matters.

### Features
- Three-lane endless dodge gameplay
- Swipe or tap controls designed for one hand
- Progressive speed and obstacle difficulty
- Coins and cosmetic character skins
- Shield and Magnet power-ups
- Near-miss bonuses and combo scoring
- One optional coin-cost Revive per run
- Seven-day Daily Reward
- Rotating daily Missions
- Personal top-10 local leaderboard
- Offline-first gameplay with no account required
- Sound, music and vibration controls

Dash Dodge stores game progress locally on your device. This release does not include online multiplayer, online leaderboards, advertising, analytics, or payment processing.
