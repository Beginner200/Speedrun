Dash Dodge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026
Short version: Dash Dodge is designed to work offline. The current release does not require an account, does not send gameplay data to a server, and does not use advertising or analytics services.

Information stored

The game stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device, including coins, best scores, owned and selected skins, daily-reward state, mission progress, settings, and the local personal leaderboard.

Information not collected

The current release does not collect or transmit contacts, location, camera data, microphone recordings, photos, account credentials, payment information, or gameplay data to a game server.

Third-party services

The game uses Phaser and Capacitor to provide the game and Android app runtime. The current release does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Vibration

If enabled in Settings, the game may use your device's vibration/haptic capability for gameplay feedback. This does not transmit information from your device.

Deleting local data

The in-game Reset Progress option removes the Dash Dodge local save data from the device.

Future changes

If a future version introduces accounts, online services, advertising, analytics, payments, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before those features are introduced.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the contact information provided on the store listing for the version of Dash Dodge you installed.
